Deadspeak

Deadspeak is a free Android app for learning to read dead languages. It teaches Latin, with Ancient Greek as a preview, through short daily practice sessions and spaced review. It is published by Magublafix Creations.

What it does

  • Teaches reading vocabulary, grammar and short texts in Latin and Ancient Greek.
  • Works fully offline. There is no Deadspeak account and no Deadspeak server.
  • Optional: sync your learning progress between your own phones through the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Sync is off by default, and the developer never receives your data.

Get the app

Deadspeak is distributed through Google Play. It is currently in closed testing.
