# Deadspeak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Deadspeak is a language-learning app for reading dead languages (currently Latin, with
Ancient Greek as a preview), published by Magublafix Creations.

## Short version

Deadspeak does not collect your personal data, and nothing is sent to the developer.
Your learning progress stays on your own device unless you choose to turn on the
optional sync to your own Google Drive, described below. With sync off, the app sends
nothing over the network.

## What the app stores

- **Learning progress** (which items you have practised and when they are due for
  review) is saved in a file in the app's private storage on your device.
- There is no Deadspeak account and no Deadspeak server. This file leaves your device
  only if you turn on sync (next section).

## Optional sync to your own Google Drive

Sync is **off by default** and happens only after you turn it on and approve Google's
permission screen.

- **What is sent:** only the progress file. In plain terms it records which words and
  chapters you have practised, when, and how many mistakes you made. It contains no
  lesson content, no translations, and no name or email address. The practice times in
  it are behavioural data about you, which is why we say so here.
- **Where it goes:** to the hidden "app data" folder of your own Google Drive (on a work
  or school account, that is your organisation's Drive). The folder is hidden from the
  Drive file list and cannot be read by other apps; Google, which stores it, can read
  it. The only permission the app asks Google for is the Drive "app data" permission
  (`drive.appdata`): it creates and updates that one file and never deletes it.
- **Who receives it:** Google stores it for you under Google's own terms and privacy
  policy. The developer never receives it. What the app keeps about your
  Google account is Google's account id and, to show which account is connected, its
  email address; both are stored only on your phone (to make sure each install syncs
  with one account) and are never uploaded.
- **What it needs:** a Google account and Google Play services on the phone. Without
  them the app works exactly as before, minus sync.
- **Stopping and deleting:** turning sync off stops the app syncing but leaves the copy
  already in your Drive, and it keeps counting toward your Drive storage. To withdraw the
  permission, use your Google Account (Security -> Third-party access). To remove the
  copy, use Google Drive Settings -> Manage apps -> Deadspeak -> Delete hidden app data;
  turn sync off on every phone first, because a phone that is still syncing uploads its
  progress again.
- **With sync off**, the app makes no network connection at all.

## What the app does not do

- It uses the internet permission **only** for the optional sync above, and for nothing
  else: no content downloads, no web views, no remote images.
- It contains **no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no
  third-party tracking** libraries.
- It does **not** ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
  photos, files, or any other personal information.
- It does **not** use Android's own cloud backup (Android auto-backup is switched off for
  this app); the only copy that can leave your phone is the sync copy you opted into.

## Losing your progress

If you never turn on sync, progress is stored only on your device and is not backed up:

- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, **deletes your
  progress on the device permanently**.
- Switching to a new phone starts you from scratch, unless you used sync, in which case
  turning it on in the new install brings your progress back.

To delete your data: uninstall the app or use Android Settings -> Apps -> Deadspeak ->
Storage -> Clear storage; and, if you used sync, also delete the hidden app data in
Google Drive as described above. We have nothing to delete on our side because we never
receive anything.

## Google Play

If you install Deadspeak through Google Play, Google processes information about the
installation (for example, that your account installed the app) under Google's own
privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Google through this.

## Children

Deadspeak is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Acknowledgements

The pronunciation clips in the app were generated offline, before release, with the
Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0 licence), using its `em_alex` voice. The
clips are bundled in the app; no speech is generated on your device and no audio is
sent anywhere.

The language illustrations on the language picker and the learning path (the laurel
wreath for Latin and the lyre player for Ancient Greek) were generated before release
with Google's Gemini image generation, then reduced to one-colour shapes. They are
bundled in the app; nothing is generated or downloaded on your device.

These notes are a courtesy; they do not change what data the app handles.

## Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data (for example, adding analytics) or sends anything
beyond the sync described above, this policy will be updated first and the change will be
stated here with a new date.
